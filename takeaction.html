Demand Congress Conduct Unannounced Inspections

People inside the Burlington ICE facility have faced prolonged detention in overcrowded conditions without beds, adequate medical care, sanitation, reliable legal access, or basic dignity. Members of Congress have tried to inspect ICE sites, but DHS and ICE have repeatedly obstructed meaningful oversight through restrictive rules and procedural delays. By law, ICE must allow for unannounced inspections. Without consistent, independent inspections, the public cannot verify what is happening inside and families are left without transparency or accountability. In response, a new group has just been formed named the Congressional ICE Accountability Coalition to push for sustained, coordinated, unannounced oversight visits and public reporting of findings.

The Immigration Justice Action Team (IJAT) is an Indivisible Mass Coalition action group coordinating a statewide effort to pressure U.S. legislators to conduct these unannounced visits. Writing Postcards from Home

Securing lasting institutional accountability depends on protecting voting rights and boosting voter turnout.

There are three options for writing postcards from home.

Email for postcard info

Email info@burlingtonindivisible.org to get details about writing postcards from home.

Pick up postcards at Wednesday Bearing Witness

You can pick up postcards in person at Wednesday Bearing Witness.

Write with established postcard organizations

Use the organization list below to choose a postcard program and get started.

  • Swing Blue Alliance: Swing Blue Alliance runs volunteer postcard programs and host networks. Their current postcard campaign focuses on Ohio Youth Voter Registration, where volunteers organize postcard parties or write postcards that help register and mobilize young voters in Ohio.

  • VoteRiders: VoteRiders is a non-partisan organization focused entirely on voter ID education and assistance. They provide the information and direct legal or financial support needed for voters to obtain required identification and confidently cast ballots. For mailings, they use Sincere: you sign up, get voter addresses from Sincere, print labels, and place labels on envelopes with a VoteRiders information sheet and a short handwritten note. You can also sign up for the Helpline Volunteer program.

  • Activate America Postcard Program: Activate America organizes volunteers to write handwritten postcards to voters in crucial districts. They use Sincere as well (see VoteRiders above for how Sincere works). You provide the postcards and complete the writing and mailing.

  • Postcards to Voters: Postcards to Voters does not rely on fixed scripts. They also use Sincere for address access (see VoteRiders above for how Sincere works). You order your own postcards, submit your draft message for approval, then receive voter addresses.

  • Center for Common Ground: The Center for Common Ground runs the Reclaim Our Vote postcard campaign, focusing heavily on fighting voter suppression in underrepresented communities. They provide materials; you write notes and addresses.

  • Progressive Turnout Project: The Progressive Turnout Project coordinates a massive Postcards to Swing States initiative to rally voters. Their program relies on evidence-based voter models, providing volunteers with clear data and localized metrics to evaluate total outreach results after election cycles close. They provide postcards for free. You write addresses, follow the script, and add postage.

  • Blue Wave Postcards: Blue Wave Postcards is a grassroots organization dedicated to increasing voter turnout through personalized, handwritten outreach. Volunteers purchase card kits at a low cost of about $0.14 per card. Most of the campaign message is pre-printed, and kits include self-stick voter address labels, which minimizes writing time.
