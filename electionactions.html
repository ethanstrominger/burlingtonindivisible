Help Voters Cure (Fix) Rejected Ballots - Virtual

Thousands of mail-in and absentee ballots are set aside each election due to technical errors like missing signatures or missing ID information. Volunteers can call voters and help them cure ballots so their votes are counted.

Visit VoPro Pros for more information. To sign up to volunteer, click here.

Find Out Voting Plans and Other Phone Banks - Virtual and In Person

At this point in the election many phone banks call voters to help them make a clear vote plan—when, where, and how they will vote—so they can participate confidently.

mobilize.us is a great way to find phone banks any day of the week, either virtual or in person. Search “GOTV2024 phone” or just “phone,” use Virtual Only or a location filter, and select a specific day. If you cannot find one that matches your criteria, email info@burlingtonindivisible.org.

Re-enroll Past Poll Observers - Virtual, Anytime

Outreach callers reconnect with past supporters, explain how poll observers protect voter access, and help people register for observer training.

Visit VoPro Pros for more information. To sign up to volunteer, click here.

Canvassing in NH and Maine - Location-Specific Action, Virtual Training

Canvass volunteers knock on doors to help likely supporters confirm their voting plans and answer general election-process questions.

Use mobilize.us to find canvasses in New Hampshire, Maine, or other locations. Search “canvass,” enter your location, and filter by day. If you cannot find a good match, email info@burlingtonindivisible.org.

Become a Trained Poll Observer in Maine

Poll observers watch voting locations to ensure procedures are followed, resources are available, and any interference or intimidation is reported right away.

Maine Democratic Party poll observer trainings are available on a recurring schedule. Sign up at mobilize.us/mainecoordinated/event/1021719/ .

Prepare for Post-Election Mobilization

Grassroots networks are preparing coordinated rapid response actions if post-election disruptions occur. Training and planning sessions are available now so volunteers can respond effectively. If you cannot find the right training or alerts, email info@burlingtonindivisible.org.
