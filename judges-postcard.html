Tell Judges to Hold ICE in Contempt

Use the addresses and script ideas to contact U.S. District of Massachusetts judges. The goal is to urge enforcement when ICE violates court orders. Postcards are provided at Bearing Witness and Justice 4 All protests outside the Burlington ICE office. If you cannot attend, either email info@burlingtonindivisible.org or download and print your own postcard.

For more background and details, visit https://tiny.cc/court

How to write and send Postcard script ideas

Tell U.S. District of Massachusetts judges to hold ICE in contempt

  1. Choose a judge.
  2. Review the scripts and choose one.
  3. Write your selected judge a postcard.
