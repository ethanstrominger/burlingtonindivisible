Welcome to Burlington Indivisible

We are a Burlington, MA community working together to defend democracy, support immigrants, and take meaningful local action through advocacy, protest, volunteering, and mutual support. Burlington Indivisible organizes postcard events and Monday/Tuesday protests in Burlington.

Whether you are joining for the first time or returning each week, you are welcome here.
