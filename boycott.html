Boycott ICE Prison Financiers

Why boycott prison-financing banks?

  • Susceptible to public opinion: Consumer-facing banks care about reputation and are often more responsive to coordinated public pressure.
  • Important to ICE: Financial institutions can enable companies that operate ICE detention centers. Cutting off that support can disrupt detention infrastructure.
