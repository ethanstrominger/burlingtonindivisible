This Week's High-Priority Phone Banks

Our VoPro Pros calling team is running phone banks for several states where results will be crucial this November. This week, we are helping Ohioans with registration issues and recruiting poll observers for Iowa. Please join us in these efforts to support battleground states! These phone banks get top ratings from our volunteers who get to speak to many grateful voters. You will receive training, a script, and support at each session. No phone banking experience is needed.

Also, note that these phone banks are open to the public, so please encourage your like-minded friends to join in.

URGENT! High-Priority Ohio (OH) Flagged Voter Phone Banks
Many voters have had their registrations flagged due to a mismatch between the OH Bureau of Motor Vehicles and the error prone Social Security Administration records. We are calling voters who have been flagged and providing them with the information they need to fix the issue before the fast-approaching October 19 deadline. Those who miss the deadline will be forced to vote a Provisional Ballot, which carries the risk of not being counted.

When:
Thursday, October 8, 6pm - 8 pm ET
Saturday, October 10, 12:30pm - 3:30pm ET
Tuesday, October 13, 6pm - 8 pm ET
Sunday, October 18, 4pm - 6pm ET

How: Sign up Mobilize link.

Help Disabled Voters in Pennsylvania

We need Designated Agents (DAs) to assist disabled voters whose mail-in ballots were rejected for technical errors. DAs help voters pick up a replacement ballot and/or return the ballot to a drop box or drop-off site for their votes to count. You do not need to be a PA resident to be a DA; anyone with access to transportation in PA can do this work.

When: Now through November 3

How: Go to the #Pennsylvania-Projects channel in Slack for more information and to sign up.
